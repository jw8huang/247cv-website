Restaurant Guest Services SMS Program (247OrQ)

Reservation confirmations and two-way guest messaging at our hospitality venue clients.

247 CV Solutions operates the 247OrQ guest-services SMS program on behalf of restaurant venues that use our hospitality platform. Guests can text a participating venue to ask questions, book, change, or cancel a reservation, and receive reservation confirmations and updates.

Text a participating venue

Hawg Heaven (Pasadena, CA): text (213) 715-0008

By texting us you agree to receive automated messages. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply.

How guests opt in

There are two guest opt-in paths, both guest-initiated:

  • Path 1 — Guest texts the venue: texting the venue's published number starts the conversation, and the first automated reply repeats the full disclosure shown above (message frequency, msg & data rates, STOP/HELP).
  • Path 2 — Guest books by phone: when a guest calls the venue to make a reservation, the venue's automated phone host asks for the guest's mobile number to send the reservation confirmation. The guest provides the number verbally and is told on the call that a confirmation text will be sent. The first text message asks the guest to reply Y to confirm or N to cancel the reservation and includes opt-out instructions (reply STOP) — no further messages are sent to guests who do not respond or who opt out. Providing a number is not a condition of booking.

Venue staff who receive operational alerts opt in separately during venue onboarding and can manage their notification preferences in-app at any time.

Program details

  • Message Types: reservation confirmations, reservation change and cancellation notices, replies to guest questions (hours, parking, menu), waitlist updates, and operational alerts to opted-in venue staff
  • Message Frequency: varies; typically 1 to 5 messages per reservation or conversation
  • Message and Data Rates: may apply — contact your wireless carrier for details
  • Opt-Out: reply STOP to any message at any time; the opt-out is honored immediately
  • Help: reply HELP to any message, or email support@247cv.com
  • Privacy: see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service below

Hiring & Onboarding SMS Program

Communications between recruiters and job applicants at our hospitality venue clients.

247 CV Solutions operates an SMS communications program on behalf of restaurant and hospitality venues that use our hiring and onboarding software. The program delivers messages to job applicants and newly hired employees regarding their application, interview scheduling, hiring decisions, and onboarding paperwork.

How recipients opt in

Opt-in occurs when an applicant submits an employment application through the QR-code-based public application form posted at participating venue locations. The form includes a required SMS-consent checkbox with full disclosure of message types, frequency, and opt-out instructions. Express written consent is captured with a per-applicant timestamp at the time of application submission. There is no website-based opt-in for this program.

How recipients opt out

Reply STOP to any message at any time. The opt-out is honored immediately and applies to all future messages from this program.

247 CV Solutions is committed to protecting the privacy of the guests, job applicants, and employees of the venues we serve.
