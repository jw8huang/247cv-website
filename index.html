Hiring & Onboarding SMS Program

Communications between recruiters and job applicants at our hospitality venue clients.

247 CV Solutions operates an SMS communications program on behalf of restaurant and hospitality venues that use our hiring and onboarding software. The program delivers messages to job applicants and newly hired employees regarding their application, interview scheduling, hiring decisions, and onboarding paperwork.

How recipients opt in

Opt-in occurs when an applicant submits an employment application through the QR-code-based public application form posted at participating venue locations. The form includes a required SMS-consent checkbox with full disclosure of message types, frequency, and opt-out instructions. Express written consent is captured with a per-applicant timestamp at the time of application submission. There is no website-based opt-in for this program.

How recipients opt out

Reply STOP to any message at any time. The opt-out is honored immediately and applies to all future messages from this program.
