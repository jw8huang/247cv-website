Hiring & Onboarding SMS Program

About this SMS Program

Communications between recruiters and job applicants at our hospitality venue clients.

247 CV Solutions operates an SMS communications program on behalf of restaurant and hospitality venues that use our hiring and onboarding software. The program delivers messages to job applicants and newly hired employees regarding their application, interview scheduling, hiring decisions, and onboarding paperwork.

How recipients opt in

Opt-in occurs when an applicant submits an employment application through the QR-code-based public application form posted at participating venue locations. The form includes a required SMS-consent checkbox with full disclosure of message types, frequency, and opt-out instructions. Express written consent is captured with a per-applicant timestamp at the time of application submission. There is no website-based opt-in for this program.

How recipients opt out

Reply STOP to any message at any time. The opt-out is honored immediately and applies to all future messages from this program.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

Introduction

247 CV Solutions is committed to protecting the privacy of job applicants and employees of the venues we serve. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our hiring and onboarding SMS program.

Information We Collect

  • Application Information: name, email, phone number, work history, and other details provided through the venue's QR-code application form
  • Communication Data: SMS messages exchanged between you and the recruiter, including timestamps and delivery status
  • Onboarding Information (if hired): information you submit through the secure onboarding form (e.g., address, tax withholding selections, signature)

SMS/Text Messaging

  • Message Frequency: Varies per applicant. Typically 3 to 10 messages from application submission through your first day of work.
  • Message and Data Rates: May apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details.

By submitting a job application via the QR-code form and checking the SMS-consent box, you consent to receive SMS messages from the venue and 247 CV Solutions about your application status, interview scheduling, hiring decisions, and (if hired) onboarding tasks. Consent is not a condition of employment.

How to Opt-In

  • Submit a job application via the QR-code application form posted at the venue location, and check the SMS-consent box on the form.

How to Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving SMS.

Or email support@247cv.com

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share information only with the venue you applied to (so they can review your application and onboard you if hired), with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as Twilio for SMS delivery), and with legal authorities when required by law. We do not share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.
